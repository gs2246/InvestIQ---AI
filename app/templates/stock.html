Trend Following

This system buys strength. It enters when RSI climbs above 60 while the weekly close is above the {{ sma_period }}-week average, and it exits when a weekly close falls below that average. The average works as a trailing stop. There is no price target.

The system is checked on Mondays, once the previous week's candle has closed. Only completed weeks are evaluated: the reading below refers to the newest completed week in the data, and prices are a one-time download, not today's market.

Value Buy — technical pullback entry

This is not fundamental value investing. It uses no P/E ratio, no book value and no company financials. “Value” here only means buying a pullback on the price chart.

Unlike Trend Following, which buys strength, this system buys weakness. When monthly RSI is between 38 and 45 and rising, a green weekly candle arms a stop-buy at that candle's high. The trade has a fixed stop at the candle's low and a defined target at the recent swing high. If the stop-buy is not triggered within two weeks, it lapses.

Only completed weeks and months are evaluated, and prices are a one-time download, not today's market.

Cup & Handle — long-term base pattern

On monthly candles, this system looks for a cup lasting at least five years: a long, rounded dip whose right rim recovers to within 8% of the left rim, followed by a short, shallow handle. The buy point is the handle's high. A stop-buy sits 4% above it, and the stop sits 24% below it.

Detection is approximate. A detected shape is only ever shown as a possible cup formation for you to review on the monthly chart. It is never a buy signal, and its price levels appear only after you confirm it.
