InvestIQ AI

Algorithms calculate; AI explains. Every price, indicator, signal and backtest on this site is worked out by fixed, checkable rules, and the same numbers come out every time. The AI never calculates anything. It only explains figures that were already worked out, and it never sees raw prices, so it cannot invent a number.

Calculated figures are printed in ink. Explanations sit in the margin in brass, like notes pencilled beside a ledger. The three trading systems come from a handwritten trading notebook. The app reports what those rules say and how they would have done in the past, never what anyone should buy or sell.
